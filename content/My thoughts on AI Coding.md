It feels like every single company right now is hyping AI as much as possible, and trying to get on the hype train to sell more stocks of their 8+ figures company to YCombinator.

### Trained on code
I've been doing a lot of AI coding for my job, we've been developing this agent assistant that is going to give suggestions to people about their finances, opportunities of investment, etc etc. And in the process I've learn about this LLM stuff. All of them are trained on data, that single-handlely might be the main reason why AI nowadays is far stronger than before.

Everything is data, from our data, to videos, podcast, music, pictures, metadata, books, ebooks, newsletters. Every single corner of the internet is being used to train Large Language Models to do x and y.

## That's the key issue
LLM's are trained in the good, and the bad, the clean and the shitty code, so yea, it can code, definitely. But at the expense of having to debug, cleanup, and redo most of the progress yourself, due to this doing good and bad code. Taking your time instead of optimizing your time.

### What do I recommend?
Code; Read; use this tools, but is just that bro, is just another tool, there are plenty of tools out there like this, it was just a fad, another bubble like the dot com bubble. So just free yourself of this thoughts. Being worried about the end of developers as we know them.

Learn the basics of software architecture, learn math, learn and comprehend what the AI code is doing, but it shouldn't replace your actual knowledge, but empower it.

And just install linux or something like that, have fun with this space, do software cuz you like it, not because it gives you money.
