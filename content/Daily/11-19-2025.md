### What's a path?
Let's imagine for a bit, you're a young fellow trying to dwell in the current job market trying to understand what should you learn; what should you go for?; what should you become?

This type of questiones are present in our search of finding meaning, that might be one of the core reasons why people are trying so hard to become x, y or z. Trying to find a reason to exist, the lack of purpose and path turns our inner ability of going `auto` mode.

#### What's the `auto` mode?
auto mode is the automatic default path, just following the current trend, going all directions expecting to find the inner goal, without actually knowing what you're good for, for example the lack of a purpose will make you be in auto mode, just doom scrolling your phone. Trying to reach that inner chemical reaction, that dopamine hit of curiosity.

What I'm trying to explain with this idea, is that if you don't know your purpose, if you don't know or think about your future, you're lost, and more in the current era of digital expansiveness.
